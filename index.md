# PillTime — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

PillTime is a medication reminder app. This policy explains, in plain language,
what the app does with your information.

## The short version

PillTime itself does not collect anything you enter. There is no account, no
sign-in and no PillTime server. Your medications, photos, reminders and history
stay on your phone.

PillTime is free and shows ads, served by Google AdMob. The ad SDK runs
separately from your medication data and never receives it.

## What the app stores, and where

All of the following is written only to PillTime's private storage area on your
device:

- The medications you add: name, strength, form, dose, instructions and any
  refill count.
- The photos you take or choose for a medication.
- Your reminder times and which medications each reminder covers.
- The record of which doses you marked as taken, skipped or missed.
- Your app settings.

No other app can read this. It is removed when you uninstall PillTime.

## Photos

PillTime asks for camera access only when you choose to take a picture of a pill
or a box, and it asks for nothing when you pick an existing picture, because
Android's photo picker hands over just the single image you selected. Pictures
are copied into the app's private storage. They are never uploaded, never shared
and never added to your gallery.

## Advertising

PillTime shows banner ads in its main screens using Google AdMob. Ads never
appear in reminder notifications or on the full-screen reminder.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and send to
Google:

- your device's advertising ID,
- approximate location derived from your IP address,
- device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
- ad interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostics.

This data is collected by Google, not by PillTime, and is handled under
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The ad SDK has no access to your medications, photos, reminders or history.

**Your choices.** If you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or a region with a
similar law, PillTime asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown,
and you can change that choice any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**.
Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads
personalisation, in your phone's **Settings → Google → Ads** (or
**Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## Backups

If you have Android's own backup turned on, your device may include PillTime's
data in the encrypted backup your phone makes to your Google account. That
backup is handled entirely by Android and Google, not by PillTime. You can turn
it off in your device settings.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show your reminders. |
| Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) | So a reminder arrives at the minute you chose, rather than whenever the system next wakes up. |
| Camera | Only when you choose to photograph a pill or a box. |
| Start at boot | To restore your reminders after the phone restarts. |
| Vibrate | To vibrate when a reminder arrives. |
| Internet, network state, advertising ID | Added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load ads. PillTime itself sends nothing. |

## Children

PillTime is not directed at children. Ad requests are not tagged as
child-directed, and ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.
